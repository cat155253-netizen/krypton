Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · 2026 · KRYPTON STUDIO

Krypton Studio ("we", "Krypton") builds premium interactive websites for companies. This policy explains what we do with the information you send through this application form.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Company name, applicant name and position, a contact email, and the project details you choose — theme, page length, style, delivery format, features, delivery speed, tip and the calculated quote. We also generate an Application No., an Application ID, and a Delivery Passcode for your submission.

HOW WE USE IT

To process your application, prepare an accurate quote and receipt, contact you about the project, and deliver the finished website. The Delivery Passcode is reserved for that delivery — it protects handover to you.

STORAGE & TRANSMISSION

Submission details are received by our local server and confirmation emails are sent through our email provider (Gmail) over encrypted connections. We keep a record for project delivery, billing and correspondence.

SHARING & SELLING

We never sell your data and never share it with third parties for marketing. It is only used to run your project and correspond with you about it.

YOUR DELIVERY PASSCODE

Keep your Delivery Passcode safe — it is required at website handover. If it is lost, reissuing a new passcode takes time and a reissue charge applies.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about your company at any time by replying to your confirmation email or writing to mrcat4005@gmail.com. We'll action it promptly.
